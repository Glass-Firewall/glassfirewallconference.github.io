Help keep Glass Firewall free

Partner support can cover the venue, food, workshop equipment, accessibility needs, and other costs that would otherwise fall on attendees. It helps make sure the event remains open to women who want to learn without worrying about the cost of participation.

Where support can go

What sponsorship can cover

Every event has different needs. We can talk through what would be most useful for the conference year you want to support.

Attendance costs

Help cover the venue, meals, accessibility services, or attendee travel.

Hands-on learning

Provide hardware kits, software access, lab infrastructure, devices, or other workshop materials.

Speakers and mentors

Support speaker travel, preparation, and materials.

Event resources

Help record sessions, publish materials, and organize follow-up resources.

More than funding

Other ways organizations can contribute

Host

Offer an accessible venue, reliable connectivity, workshop rooms, or operational support in a future city.

Teach

Invite practitioners who can teach a clear session for beginners.

Recruit volunteers

Encourage employees or members to mentor, facilitate labs, welcome attendees, or support logistics.

Share the event

Tell schools, nonprofits, student groups, and professional networks about the conference.
