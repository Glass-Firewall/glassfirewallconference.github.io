There are many ways to help

We need technical instructors, but we also need hosts, mentors, event volunteers, photographers, accessibility support, and people who can help first-time attendees find their way around.

Most helpful right now

We especially value volunteers who can support onboarding, workshop flow, accessibility, mentoring, and community outreach.

Beginner-friendly support

People who can welcome first-time attendees, explain logistics, and make a new learner feel comfortable are invaluable.

Technical and practical help

We can also use people who can support labs, speak on technical topics, or help facilitate hands-on sessions.

Community reach

If you know student groups, professional networks, or local communities we should connect with, that helps as much as any single volunteer role.

Choose your contribution

Ways to get involved

Needs change each year. Tell us what you can help with, and the organizing team will let you know where it fits.

Volunteer on site

Help the day run smoothly and make attendees feel welcome.

  • Registration and wayfinding
  • Room and workshop support
  • Speaker assistance
  • Setup and breakdown

Teach or facilitate

Teach what you know in a way that beginners can follow.

  • Technical talks
  • Guided workshops
  • Career panels
  • CTF and lab support

Mentor and encourage

Answer questions and help attendees figure out what to learn next.

  • Answer beginner questions
  • Review learning goals
  • Share career perspective
  • Make community introductions

Spread the word

Help the event reach people who may not see cybersecurity as an option yet.

  • Share registration
  • Partner with student groups
  • Invite local communities
  • Amplify learning resources

Support storytelling

Document the event while respecting attendee consent.

  • Event photography
  • Session recaps
  • Speaker interviews
  • Resource organization

Sponsor or partner

Help cover the costs of a free conference and reach more attendees.

  • Venue and food support
  • Workshop materials
  • Travel assistance
  • Community outreach
When you contact us

What to include when you reach out

Your contribution

Describe the role, topic, resource, or support you would like to offer.

Your experience

Share relevant background, especially experience teaching or supporting beginner audiences.

Your availability

Include your city, time zone, and whether you can help before, during, or after the conference.

Your accessibility needs

Tell us what would help you participate fully as a volunteer, speaker, or mentor.

For community organizations

Universities, student groups, nonprofits, professional associations, and local security groups can recommend speakers, share the event, provide volunteers, or host follow-up activities. Tell us who your organization serves and how you would like to work together.

Ready to help?

Send a short introduction and tell us how you would like to help. If you are interested in sponsorship, take a look at the partner page first.
